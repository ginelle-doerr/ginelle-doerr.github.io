*2025 · ArcGIS Pro • Cartographic Design*

A cartographic design project: a map of Crater Lake National Park for visitors going to the park for a day.

The map shows what a day visitor needs, including park roads, trailheads and hiking trails, visitor centers, viewpoints, picnic areas, and campgrounds, over shaded relief so the caldera and the surrounding terrain read clearly.
